Model assumptions & verification

Euler–Bernoulli 2D frame elements, three degrees of freedom per node, fixed bases and rigid beam–column joints. Rectangular solid sections; one elastic modulus for all members. Braces are axial-only and carry tension and compression. No releases, shear deformation, cracked stiffness, buckling, P–Δ, torsion, seismic dynamics, slab or foundation modelling.

Floor area loads become uniform beam loads using the entered tributary width. Bracing self-weight is lumped at its ends. All combinations here are unfactored studies, not Eurocode design combinations. No strength or reinforcement check is performed.

Analytical checks: cantilever displacement/rotation, uniformly loaded beam reactions/rotations, axial shortening, and frame equilibrium. Independent solver benchmarking remains pending.

02 / Floor response

Displacement and drift use the mean horizontal joint displacement at each floor; no rigid-diaphragm constraint is imposed. Drift is the difference between adjacent floor means divided by storey height. No pass/fail threshold is applied.
