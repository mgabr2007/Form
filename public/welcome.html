Know the limits.

Analysis and validation

Field accuracy and live-site deployment remain unverified.

Use results for early design exploration. Irradiation is incident solar energy, not PV electricity. Sky metrics do not establish indoor daylight compliance. Check inputs, sampling and weather provenance before drawing conclusions.

Structural exploration is not a safety verdict. Environmental and structural tools are not a coupled simulation; the comparison dashboard does not compare structural runs.

Validation and limitations ↗
Saving and exports

Local saves belong to this browser. Sign in through Projects to explicitly save a cloud snapshot; synchronisation is not automatic. Structural runs are separate from cloud snapshots. Keep a JSON backup or a FORM package for imported models.

IFC and GLB exports are conceptual geometry, not construction-ready BIM models. Drawings and data can also be exported where available.

The studio requires WebGL2. Videos are silent and can be paused; automatic playback respects reduced-motion and data-saving preferences.
